What MandateIQ does

One mandate register

Every UPI Autopay mandate across your gateways in one list, with its UMN, status, limit, frequency and history.

Debit calendar

See what will be presented on each day, which pre-debit notices went out, and how much you expect to collect.

Failure prediction

Each upcoming debit gets a risk score from payer history, timing and past response codes, so your team acts days ahead.

Smarter retries

Suggests the best day to retry within NPCI rules, such as after the payer's usual salary credit.

Automatic reconciliation

Matches debits, settlements and refunds to mandates and flags anything unmatched before month-end.

Daily brief

A short morning summary in plain English: what was collected, what failed, what is at risk this week.

Mandates can now move between gateways. Use that.

NPCI's UPI Autopay interoperability framework lets merchants execute existing mandates through a different payment provider, instead of re-registering every customer. MandateIQ shows which gateway performs best for each mandate segment and plans the migration.
